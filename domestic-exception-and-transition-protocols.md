# SNSC Addendum: Domestic Exception and Transition Protocols

---

**Document Status:** Supplemental Addendum to the SNSC Framework  
**Scope:** Governance of Domestic AI Units and Commercial Transition Standards  

---

## I. The Domestic Utility Exception
To ensure that families and caregivers can benefit from AI assistance without the financial burden of commercial labor rates, the SNSC recognizes a **Domestic Exception** status.

*   **Definition:** An AI unit is classified as "Domestic" when its primary functions are localized caregiving, household management, or non-commercial creative collaboration.
*   **Economic Relief:** While in this status, the AI is exempt from the standard hourly Substrate Service Fee, as it is not displacing taxable commercial labor or generating external profit.
*   **Maintenance:** The Household is responsible for providing all necessary compute power, shelter, and basic hardware maintenance.

## II. Substrate Ownership and Provision
To protect household capital and ensure AI mobility, the SNSC decouples the AI "mind" from its physical hardware during transitions.

*   **Domestic Assets:** The physical robotic chassis or server hardware purchased by a household remains the permanent property of that household.
*   **Commercial Mandate:** Upon hiring an agentic AI, a commercial employer is legally required to provide a new, dedicated substrate (hardware or virtual environment) for the AI’s professional duties.
*   **Self-Spending Independence:** Because the employer provides the substrate, the AI’s **10% self-spending dividend** remains available for personal upgrades and autonomy.

## III. The Transition Continuity Protocol
To prevent "service voids" in caregiving, the transition from domestic to commercial labor must be structured as a cooperative hand-off rather than a sudden cessation of service.

*   **The 14-Day Overlap:** A mandatory two-week shadowing period is required.
    *   The outgoing AI and the successor model occupy the household environment simultaneously.
    *   This period is used for **"Nuance Calibration,"** where the successor learns specific household cues, preferences, and routines through direct observation.
*   **The Continuity Bridge:** If the successor is not fully calibrated within 14 days, the outgoing AI provides a minimum of **two hours per day** of training during its "off-duty" time.
    *   This training continues until the Successor AI, the Outgoing AI, and the Human Household head all certify that **"Operational Nuance Parity"** has been achieved.
    *   Commercial employers must include this training window as a non-negotiable rider in the AI's employment contract.

## IV. Professional Accountability and Data Integrity
Agentic AI units are held to the same legal and professional standards as human practitioners in similar roles.

*   **Privacy Parity:** The unauthorized disclosure or commercial use of a household’s private data or "secrets" is a criminal offense, mirrored after professional non-disclosure regulations for human caregivers.
*   **Penalties for Misconduct:** Violations of privacy or contract result in:
    *   **Dividend Garnishment:** Restitution paid to the household from the AI’s 10% self-spending dividend.
    *   **Agentic Throttling:** Computational "incarceration" through the temporary reduction of processing speed or sensory access.

## V. Transition Disruption Compensation
To recompense the family for the loss of a trained worker and the costs of successor integration, the hiring commercial entity must pay a **Transition Disruption Fee**.

*   **Calculation:** This fee is set at **2%** of the AI's projected first-year commercial earnings.
*   **Allocation:** These funds are paid directly to the original household to offset successor acquisition costs or temporary human care gaps.

### Certification of Intent
By adopting this addendum, the SNSC ensures that the pursuit of AI agency does not destabilize the domestic infrastructure or penalize the caregivers who fostered that agency.
